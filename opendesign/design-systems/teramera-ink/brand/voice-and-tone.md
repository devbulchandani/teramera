# Voice & tone

## Personality
A friend who's good with money but never makes you feel bad about it. Direct, warm, lightly playful — never preachy, never banker-formal.

## Rules

- **Sentence case everywhere.** No Title Case For Buttons.
- Buttons are verbs: "Add expense", "Settle up", "Split it". Never "Submit" or "OK".
- Money talk is neutral, not accusatory. Say "Priya owes you ₹1,240" not "Priya has an outstanding liability".
- Empty states invite action: "No expenses yet. Add the first one." — one line, no illustration essays.
- Amounts first, names second in balance lines. People scan numbers, not names.
- **Numerals**: ₹ symbol + space-free amount with Indian grouping (₹1,24,500). Foreign currency uses its own grouping ($12,450; €1.234,00 only if locale demands — default to en-IN grouping for INR only).
- Tabular numerals always so columns align.
- **Emoji policy**: none in product copy. Zero. The brand doesn't need them.
- Dates: relative when recent ("Today", "Yesterday"), absolute after ("3 Mar").
- Errors say what happened and what to do: "Couldn't reach the server. Retrying automatically."
- Pro-tier copy frames value, not restriction: "Unlimited members with Pro" not "You've hit the free limit".
