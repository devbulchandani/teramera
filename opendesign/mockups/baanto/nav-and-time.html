  • Selection follows the destination hierarchy, so Groups stays lit on a group screen.
  • The active icon is tinted teal, not just its label.
  • Labels move from 11px to 12px and the bar gets a 1px top hairline.

Time formats

  • Today 4:12 pm — same day
  • Yesterday 9:40 am — previous day
  • 3 Mar — this year
  • 3 Mar 2026, 2:05 pm — any other year
  • Updated 2m ago — sync freshness on the Home hero
  • Days are grouped by calendar date, never by the formatted label.
  • Numbers stay tabular so columns do not dance.
