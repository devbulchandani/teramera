Baanto

The teramera mascot. A split coin spirit — half mera, half tere — who does the maths without ever making money feel like a problem. Quiet companion, not a commentator.

Meet Baanto

tere | mera

A rupee coin that split down the middle and became two friends. The teal half is what you paid; the violet half is what you owe. It is the only character in the product — it shows up when the screen is otherwise empty, and it stays quiet.

  • Name: Baanto — from baantna, to divide or share.
  • Role: marks empty, loading and confirmation moments.
  • Voice: none. Copy sits beside it, never in its mouth.

Rules

Do

  • Keep the split vertical-ish (105°), teal on the left.
  • Use cream for eyes and mouth — never ink on the accents.
  • Let it sit beside copy, aligned to the top third.
  • Animate at most 380ms, spring easing.

Don’t

  • No gradients across the two hues.
  • No emoji faces, no extra limbs, no blush.
  • Don’t make it talk or judge spending.
  • Don’t place it on busy photography.

Motion

Idle2.4° sway, 4s loop, ease-in-out. Coin bobs 2px.
ThinkingCoin orbits the body once per 3s; eyes track it.
PleasedSingle squash-stretch, 380ms spring; coin flips once.
Apologetic1.5° droop, 200ms; no loop. Coin lowers 4px.
EnteringFade + 8px rise, 260ms. Never bounces into view.
NoteThe 380ms cap applies to transitions; ambient idle/thinking loops are exempt.
