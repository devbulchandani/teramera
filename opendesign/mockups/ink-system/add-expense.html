Why this replaces the old sheet

The old add-expense was one long scrolling sheet: keypad, title, payer, split type, raw values, preview and validation all competing at once. This version makes one decision per screen and keeps the amount visible on ink.

  • Ink header holds the amount, group and mascot — the three things you check constantly.
  • Keypad keys are real cards (56px targets), not floating numerals.
  • Split is four tiles with a glyph, not four chips.
  • Review shows the exact per-person share before saving, with Baanto pleased.
  • One CTA per step, always in the same place, always peach.
  • Mascot stays in the header on all three steps — not a 2-second loading flash.

Prototype: type an amount, pick a payer and split, then continue to the review.
