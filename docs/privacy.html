Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

What we collect

  • Your Google account details — name, email address and profile picture — when you sign in with Google.
  • Your phone number — only if you choose phone-number sign-in.
  • Expense data you enter — groups you create, expenses, amounts, who paid, who participated, and settlements.

How we use it

  • To create your account and let friends find and add you to groups.
  • To show balances, simplified debts and activity to members of the same group.
  • To send you transactional emails (e.g. group invites) when requested by a friend adding you to a group.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your personal data with advertisers or third parties for marketing.
  • We don't move real money — teramera only records who owes whom.

Where your data lives

Data is stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure (D1 database) and is visible only to members of the groups you belong to.

Deleting your data
